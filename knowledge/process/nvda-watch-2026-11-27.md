## 4. The security story — reported, NOT established

CNBC, 2026-11-23, reports the board voted to reinstate Huang after
assurances from President Trump that he had not broken US national
security laws. **I could not verify this from any primary source.** The
8-K states no reason. No regulator filing, no 8-K and no NVIDIA newsroom
item resolving the matter appeared in the search.

The same CNBC piece reports that board chair Harvey Jones told analysts on
the earnings call that the board did **"not intend to reinstate Jensen as
CEO"** — days before it did exactly that.

Also from the search, both reported and unverified:
- FT reported the board had approached Arm CEO Rene Haas as a candidate
- On 2026-11-13 the board told CNBC it had **not yet selected a search firm**

Treat the whole Trump-assurance account as reported. A political assurance
is not a legal finding, and the original allegation — that one of the
relationships involved a woman US officials have described as a Chinese
asset — remains, as of 2026-11-12, reported and not established.

## The agreed three-condition test

The test was: permanent CEO **and** the security question resolved as
narrow **and** one clean quarter showing customers and bookings intact.

1. **Permanent CEO named — met on the letter only.** The chair is a
   permanent CEO, not an interim. But the board accepted a resignation,
   told analysts it would not reverse it, and reversed it the next day
   with no stated reason. That is a fact about governance; it does not
   settle the condition in spirit.
2. **Security question resolved as narrow — NOT met.** No primary source.
3. **One clean quarter — partially.** EPS beat 8.5%. But the guidance and
   the bookings/customer commentary are exactly what I cannot read, and
   the stock fell 2.89% on the close into that beat, which says the market
   found something it disliked in the part I cannot see.

**1 of 3 on the letter, 0 of 3 cleanly.** No verdict, no proposal —
Waldo's call.
