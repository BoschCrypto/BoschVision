## Why "positive operating cash flow" is not the bull case it looks like

OCF +$529m against a net loss of −$263m implies ~$790m/quarter of non-cash
charges, overwhelmingly depreciation on a $20.6bn asset base. For a building
that gap is an accounting artefact. For GPUs it is not — they genuinely lose
most of their value in 3–5 years. EBITDA and OCF flatter GPU-heavy businesses
structurally. The depreciation here is a real economic cost, so OCF overstates
earning power.

Trailing four quarters of net income: −263, −684, −248, −155 = **−$1.35bn**,
against a $5.45bn market cap. Most of that is non-cash, but per the point
above, "non-cash" is not the same as "not real".

## Verdict

Not a buy, and the 82% fall is not an overreaction.

The market is not repricing IREN's earnings — it is repricing the probability
that $18.9bn of committed spending gets financed. That is a binary funding
question, not a valuation question, and P/B 1.03 is no support when the
equity is a $5.45bn residual claim sitting on top of a $15.5bn liability
stack with $18.9bn of further obligations behind it.

Both legs that could save it are outside the company's control: the credit
market reopening for GPU paper, and AI compute demand converting the
remaining 91% of that capex into signed contracts.

If BTC (at $60.7k on 2026-11-25) and the AI capex cycle both recover, this is
a multi-bagger from here. That is a genuine possibility and should be said
plainly. But it is a leveraged option on two macro variables, not an
investment in a business at a discount.

Against the measured rules in this project it fails on every axis:
- single name from a screen (rule: never)
- small cap (IWM beat SPY in 0 of 57 rolling 3-year windows)
- concentration measured worse at every level tested
- hurdle is VTI, not zero
- position cap is 10% of TOTAL, and Robinhood held $500.24 as of 2026-11-19

## What I did not examine

- **The debt maturity ladder** (when the $9.9bn actually comes due) — decisive
  for the solvency question and not in the facts pulled
- Covenants on the GPU financings
- Power contract economics and the specific site pipeline
- Identity and credit quality of Customers A/B/C
- Whether any of the $18.95bn of commitments has been cancelled or
  renegotiated since 2026-09-30 (the 10-Q notes cancellation flexibility
  "subject to contractual terms that may include cancellation charges or
  reimbursement of supplier costs")

That last one matters most. If a material slice of the $18.9bn is cancellable
cheaply, the bear case weakens considerably.
